# AI-Based Smart Food Waste Management System

## Project Better Tomorrow – C29 Batch

### Project Overview

Food wastage is a common problem in colleges, hostels, canteens,
restaurants and other food-serving environments.

Our project proposes an AI-Based Smart Food Waste Management System
to monitor food waste, analyse consumption patterns and provide
intelligent recommendations to reduce unnecessary food wastage.

### Problem Statement

Food-serving places often prepare food based on estimated demand.
When the actual consumption is lower than expected, excess food may
be wasted.

There is a need for a smart system that can analyse food consumption
patterns and help users make better decisions about food preparation
and management.

### Proposed Solution

The proposed system uses Artificial Intelligence to analyse food
consumption and waste-related data.

The system aims to:

- Monitor food waste
- Record daily food consumption
- Analyse consumption patterns
- Predict future food requirements
- Identify frequently wasted food items
- Provide alerts and recommendations
- Generate food waste reports
- Help reduce unnecessary food preparation

### Design Thinking Process

#### Empathize

We identify the difficulties faced by students, canteen users and
food-service staff related to food wastage.

#### Define

Users need a simple and intelligent system that can help them
understand food consumption and reduce unnecessary food waste.

#### Ideate

AI is used as a brainstorming partner to generate possible solutions
and identify useful features for the proposed system.

#### Prototype

A low-to-mid fidelity prototype is developed to demonstrate the
proposed smart food waste management system.

#### Validate

The prototype will be tested with real users and their feedback will
be collected to improve the solution.

### Key Features

1. Food waste recording
2. Food consumption tracking
3. AI-based demand prediction
4. Waste analysis
5. Smart alerts
6. Food item-wise reports
7. Recommendation system
8. Monitoring dashboard

### Expected Outcome

The system aims to help food-serving environments make better
