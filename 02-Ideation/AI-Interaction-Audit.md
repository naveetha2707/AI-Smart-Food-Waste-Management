# AI Interaction Audit

## Project Title

AI-Based Smart Food Waste Management System

## Purpose of Using AI

AI was used as a brainstorming partner during the Ideation stage to
generate possible solutions for reducing food waste.

## AI Prompts and Decisions

### Prompt 1
Suggest innovative solutions for reducing food waste in college
canteens.

### AI Suggestions
- Food waste monitoring
- Demand prediction
- Smart alerts
- Food donation system
- Consumption tracking

### Decision
Adopted.

### Reason
These ideas are relevant to our problem and can be developed into
a practical prototype.

---

### Prompt 2
Suggest AI features for a smart food waste management system.

### AI Suggestions
- AI-based demand prediction
- Waste pattern analysis
- Recommendation system
- Automated alerts
- Dashboard

### Decision
Adopted.

### Reason
These features can help users understand food consumption and
manage food preparation more effectively.

---

### Prompt 3
Suggest advanced features for the project.

### AI Suggestions
Some suggestions required complex AI models and large datasets.

### Decision
Rejected.

### Reason
They were beyond the scope of our current prototype and available
resources.

## Final Selected Features

- Food waste recording
- Consumption tracking
- AI-based demand prediction
- Waste analysis
- Smart alerts
- Recommendation system
- Monitoring dashboard

## Human Decision Making

AI suggestions were reviewed by the project team. The final features
were selected based on relevance, feasibility and the project scope.
