# User Validation and Feedback

## Project

AI-Based Smart Food Waste Management System

## Purpose

The prototype was shown to real users to understand whether the
proposed system is useful, understandable and practical.

## Tester 1

**Role:** College Student

**Feedback:**
The system can help students and canteen users understand food
wastage and consumption.

**Suggested Improvement:**
Add simple notifications about food waste.

---

## Tester 2

**Role:** College Student

**Feedback:**
The dashboard should clearly show the amount of food wasted.

**Suggested Improvement:**
Add charts and visual representations of food waste.

---

## Tester 3

**Role:** Canteen User

**Feedback:**
The system could help identify which food items are wasted more often.

**Suggested Improvement:**
Add food-item-wise waste analysis.

---

## Changes Planned After Validation

Based on the feedback, the prototype can be improved by:

- Adding waste notifications
- Adding charts and graphs
- Adding food-item-wise analysis
- Making the dashboard easier to understand
- Improving the AI recommendation section

## Validation Status

Prototype validation is in progress.
