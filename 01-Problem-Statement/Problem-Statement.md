# Problem Statement

## Project Title

AI-Based Smart Food Waste Management System

## Problem

Food wastage is a common problem in colleges, hostels, canteens and
other food-serving environments. Food is often prepared based on
estimated demand, and excess food may be wasted when actual
consumption is lower than expected.

## User Need

Students and food-service staff need a simple smart system to monitor
food waste, understand consumption patterns and make better decisions
about food preparation.

## Defined Problem Statement

How might we use Artificial Intelligence to monitor food waste,
analyse food consumption patterns and predict food requirements so
that food-serving environments can reduce avoidable food wastage?

## Target Users

- College students
- Canteen staff
- Hostel food-service staff
- Restaurant staff
- Food-service managers

## Project Goal

To develop an AI-based smart system that helps monitor food waste,
analyse consumption patterns and support better food planning.
